#PROJECT
PR:PROJECT_TITLE                 	Comprehensive absolute quantitative targeted metabolomics analysis of a
PR:PROJECT_TITLE                 	sweet-waxy double recessive maize mutant with enhanced kernel filling
PR:PROJECT_TYPE                  	Metabolomics
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	This project aims to characterize the metabolic basis for increased endosperm
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	content in a sweet-waxy double recessive maize mutant (M4-6). Using a targeted
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	metabolomics approach with UHPLC-QTRAP MS/MS (AB SCIEX 6500+) on HILIC and C18
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	columns in positive and negative ion switch mode, we quantified 337 metabolites
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	in the endosperm of both mutant and wild-type at 20 days after pollination (n=3
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	per group). The mutant displayed a 41.4% increase in dry grain weight,
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	accompanied by elevated levels of soluble sugars (43.4%) and sucrose (21.2%).
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	Metabolomic profiling identified 68 differential metabolites (p&lt;0.05),
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	indicating enhanced turnover of carbon compounds, with depletion of
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	branched-chain amino acid degradation intermediates (3-methyl-2-oxobutanoic
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	acid, methylmalonic acid, ketoleucine, 3-methyl-2-oxovaleric acid) and
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	accumulation of metabolites such as β-alanine and tartaric acid. Pathway
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	enrichment analysis revealed significant alterations in
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	valine/leucine/isoleucine degradation (ko00280), glyoxylate/dicarboxylate
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	metabolism (ko00630), and β-alanine metabolism (ko00410). This dataset provides
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	a high-resolution metabolic profile of the mutant endosperm and offers insights
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	into the metabolic reprogramming associated with enhanced kernel filling.

#STUDY
ST:STUDY_TITLE                   	Targeted metabolomics of sweet-waxy double recessive maize endosperm reveals
ST:STUDY_TITLE                   	enhanced carbon flux and amino acid degradation in a kernel-filling mutant
ST:STUDY_TYPE                    	Targeted MS
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	This study performed a targeted, absolute quantitative metabolomics analysis on
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	maize (Zea mays) endosperm from a sweet-waxy double recessive mutant (M4-6, n=3)
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	and its wild-type (n=3) at 20 days after pollination (DAP). Metabolites were
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	extracted using a cold methanol/acetonitrile/water (2:2:1, v/v) method and
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	analyzed by UHPLC-QTRAP MS/MS (AB SCIEX 6500+) with both HILIC and C18 columns
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	in positive and negative ion switch mode using MRM acquisition. Of the 337
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	metabolites quantified, 68 showed significant differences (p&lt;0.05), with 15
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	upregulated and 53 downregulated in the mutant. Key findings include depletion
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	of branched-chain amino acid degradation intermediates (3-methyl-2-oxobutanoic
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	acid, methylmalonic acid, ketoleucine, 3-methyl-2-oxovaleric acid) and
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	accumulation of β-alanine and tartaric acid. Differential metabolites were
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	enriched in pathways including valine/leucine/isoleucine degradation (ko00280),
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	glyoxylate/dicarboxylate metabolism (ko00630), and β-alanine metabolism
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	(ko00410). The results demonstrate enhanced carbon flux through catabolic
ST:STUDY_SUMMARY                 	pathways in the mutant endosperm.
